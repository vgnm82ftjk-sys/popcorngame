POPCORNROYALE
A fan tribute to the Google Doodle «Celebrating Popcorn».
Be a tiny kernel on a giant frying pan — outrun Butter, Salt, Fire and the Magnetron, and be the last kernel that never pops.
MATCH SETUP
Add friends on the same keyboard, then fill the pan with bots and start the popcorn battle.
Game mode

Players

Bots 7
P1 WASD / Arrows  ·  P2 T G B H  ·  P3 I J K L
Each stage lasts 40s and the arena grows +20% after every stage.
Clear a stage and your team earns a +1 heart.
HOW TO PLAY
  • You are a corn kernel on a giant frying pan. Don't get popped.
  • Move with WASD / arrows — no abilities, pure dodging.
  • Four bosses attack in order: 🧈 Butter → 🧂 Salt → 🔥 Fire → 📡 Magnetron.
  • Survive the 40s timer of each stage to advance. The pan gets +20% bigger every stage.
  • You have 5 hearts. A hit costs one. Popcorn = elimination.
  • Grab the Golden Kernel in the center to heal a heart.
  • Outlast every other kernel — or survive all four bosses — to be crowned.
